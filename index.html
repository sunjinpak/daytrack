Daytrack

Your whole day, on one map. Daytrack records where you go all day, every day, with no start button. Each day becomes one track you can view on a map and export as GPX.

For runners, walkers and travelers who want a complete GPS log without thinking about it.

Download on the App Store

How it works

  • Open it once. Allow location access (choose Always), and recording starts. There is nothing to start or stop.
  • One track per day. Each day is saved as its own track with distance and duration.
  • Save interval. Drag a slider to save a point every 5 seconds up to once an hour. GPS always runs at full accuracy while recording.
  • Schedule. Choose the days to record and, for each day, hours not to record, such as 11 PM to 7 AM. Outside the schedule nothing is saved.
  • Export GPX. Open a day and tap Export GPX to send it to Strava, Google Earth, a photo app, an AI assistant or anywhere else that reads GPX.
  • Your data stays on your iPhone. No account, no server, no ads, no tracking.

Help

Recording stopped. What do I check?

Open iOS Settings › Daytrack › Location and make sure it is set to Always with Precise Location on. Also check that the app is not paused (the main screen shows Recording in red) and not off by schedule. If you are not sure what you changed, tap Reset settings at the bottom of the main screen: Daytrack goes back to recording every day, all day, every 10 seconds, and your saved tracks are kept.

Does it keep recording after I close the app or restart my iPhone?

Yes, with Always permission. iOS wakes Daytrack again when you start moving. After a restart, recording resumes once iOS relaunches the app, which can take a few minutes of movement.

How much battery does it use?

Daytrack keeps GPS on at full accuracy, so it uses more battery than most apps. Continued use of GPS running in the background can dramatically decrease battery life. When you stay in one place for more than five minutes, Daytrack slows its updates until you move again. Tap Pause when you do not need a record.

Where are my files?

In the Files app › On My iPhone › Daytrack. Each day is a CSV file (time, latitude, longitude, altitude, accuracy, speed) that opens in Numbers or Excel. GPX files are made when you tap Export GPX.

Can I delete a day?

Swipe left on a day in the list. Today's track cannot be deleted while it is still recording.

Can it add locations to my photos?

Daytrack does not change your photos. Export the day's GPX and use a photo app or an AI assistant that can match photo times to a GPX track.
