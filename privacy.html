Privacy Policy

Daytrack for iPhone. Effective November 24, 2026.

Summary

Daytrack does not collect, store or share any of your data. The developer has no server and cannot see your location or your tracks.

Location data

With your permission, Daytrack reads your iPhone's location, including in the background, to record the route you travel each day. The points are saved only in files on your iPhone (in the Files app under On My iPhone › Daytrack). They are never sent to the developer or anyone else, and never used for advertising.

Sharing

Nothing leaves your iPhone unless you choose to. When you tap Export GPX, iOS shows the share sheet and the file goes only where you send it.

No tracking

Daytrack has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no account. Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash and usage statistics if you have chosen to share them with app developers in iOS Settings.

Deleting your data

Swipe left on a day to delete it, or delete the app to remove all of its files.

Children

Daytrack is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: runsyncsupport@gmail.com
